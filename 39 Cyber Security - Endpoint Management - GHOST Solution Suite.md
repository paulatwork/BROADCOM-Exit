## Broadcom Product Description (Key Features) (This is important to get right)

Symantec Ghost Solution Suite handles imaging, deployment and migration of desktops, laptops and servers. Release 3.3.13 is the latest listed in TechDocs (page updated 9 June 2026). Its feature list could not be retrieved, so the features below are carried forward from Broadcom's product description.

Key features:

1. **Disk imaging** - Centralised imaging and cloning.
2. **OS deployment and migration** - Provisioning and migration across hardware.
3. **Remote command execution** - Run commands on managed machines.
4. **Recovery** - Restore devices from images.

## Analyst Cautions and Industry Findings - Related to the Legacy Broadcom Product. Include newest findings on Broadcom exist strategies

No independent Gartner, Forrester or IDC analyst coverage specific to GHOST Solution Suite was identified in this research, and no dedicated Gartner Peer Insights product page for it could be located either; this is stated plainly rather than inferring analyst sentiment that has not been verified. Broadcom does publish its own End-of-Life schedule for GHOST Solution Suite versions on its knowledge base, but that article lists no dates and Broadcom is still issuing releases (Ghost Solution Suite 3.3.13, documentation updated 9 June 2026, with about one release a year), so a per-version end-of-life schedule should not be read as confirmation that the product is being wound down.

The broader, verifiable industry trend is that thick-image, disk-cloning-based OS deployment is being retired across the industry generally (not specific to Broadcom) in favour of cloud-native, zero-touch provisioning, because thick imaging does not suit remote/hybrid workforces or modern hardware abstraction models; this is a general endpoint-management industry trend rather than a Broadcom- or GHOST-specific analyst finding.

Enterprises retiring GHOST Solution Suite are generally adopting Windows Autopilot (paired with Microsoft Intune), IBM MaaS360, Apple Business Manager (for Apple estates), or Tanium Provision.

Sources: Broadcom Knowledge Base, 'Ghost Solution Suite End of Life Schedules' (knowledge.broadcom.com); no independent analyst coverage of this product was found in this research.
