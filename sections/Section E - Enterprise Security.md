
## Category

Enterprise Security

## Content

A modern enterprise security solution is an integrated set of controls that protects identities, data, infrastructure and AI systems across on-premises, hybrid and multicloud environments, and applies common policy, telemetry and audit functions across all of them. It replaces separate point products and a compliance focused operating model with a coordinated capability that identifies exposure, detects threats, responds to incidents and produces evidence of control effectiveness for regulators and internal governance.

Typically provides four categories of service across the enterprise.

- **Real-time, operational threat detection and response**. Log, flow, endpoint and network telemetry is collected into a single analyst workflow. Detection is prioritised by risk, and response actions are orchestrated through that workflow.

- **Data security**. Sensitive data is discovered and classified, activity against data stores is monitored, and the security posture of those stores is assessed on a continuing basis. These controls apply whether data is held in client managed infrastructure or in SaaS environments.

- **Identity and access management**. Access for employees, contractors, consumers and devices is governed through a single identity framework covering legacy and cloud applications.

- **AI governance**. AI models, applications and autonomous agents as an evolving type of IT system, require specialised attention, to ensure are adequatly  inventoried and monitored throughout their lifecycle for accuracy, drift, bias and explainability.
