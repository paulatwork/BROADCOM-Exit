
## Category

02 - IT Service Management (ITSM)

## Content

The disciplined, standardised practice of designing, delivering and operating IT systems with the objective of aligning those services to business requirements and enabling operational availability. But many organistaions have realised traditional ITSM is not longer enough as most organisations still struggle with a legacy estate that is fragmented, lacking IT/OT asset visibility; based on separated ITSM and ITAM platforms; and siloed across in IT and operational technology teams.

"According to Deloitte's Global ITAM research, fewer than 40% of organizations have modernized their IT asset management processes for cloud-first environments."

IBM recognises that most customers use ServiceNow as the system-of-record for ITSM workflow, ticketing, CMDB, and process automation. IBM does not try to replace that. Instead, IBM extends ITSM in the two places where ServiceNow stops short — the operational intelligence layer (AIOps + observability), and the automated remediation layer.

**Deep AIOps and root-cause intelligence — with IBM Cloud Pak for AIOps**. ServiceNow has an AIOps/ITOM module (alert clustering, log/trace ingestion, anomaly detection), but it is oriented toward feeding its own workflow engine. IBM Cloud Pak for AIOps goes deeper on the diagnostic side: 
- Correlates signals across the entire tech stack — network, cloud, infrastructure and application monitoring tools — to eliminate manual "war rooms" and cut MTTR.
- Hybrid AI approach combining traditional ML with generative AI — not just detecting patterns but surfacing contextual insight and recommended actions.
- Rich correlation methods to compliment ServiceNow - temporal, scope-based, topology-based, seasonality, probable-cause and golden-signal analysis.

**Automated, full-stack observability and remediation — with IBM Instana.** 

- 1-second data granularity and automatic discovery of 300+ technologies with no manual instrumentation — every metric, every request, no coverage gaps.
- Agentic AI investigation — one click launches a full root-cause investigation, then generates a step-by-step runbook
- Turns diagnosis into executable remediation — auto-generates a Bash script or Ansible playbook for each step, exportable to GitHub/GitLab for review and deployment, with the human keeping control of what actually runs; and auto-document the whole investigation — hypotheses, evidence, reasoning — producing an auditable postmortem/compliance narrative automatically.
