
## Category

Application Lifecycle Management (ALM) -> Digital Engineering

## Content

The set of software and practices used to monitor, analyse, maintain and automate the technology infrastructure and applications that deliver business services. The objective is to maintain service availability and performance at a controlled operating cost. ITOM covers the technical operation of systems. IT Service Management (ITSM) covers the processes and records that govern that work, such as incidents, changes and requests.

A modern ITOM capability consists of five connected functions.

1. **Observability**. Collection and analysis of telemetry (metrics, events, logs and traces) across applications, infrastructure, networks and cloud services.
2. **Event intelligence (AIOps)**. Correlation of alerts, noise reduction and identification of probable root cause using statistical and machine learning methods.
3. **Automation and orchestration**. Scheduling of workloads and batch processes, runbook execution and automated remediation across on premises and cloud environments.
4. **Discovery and configuration data**. Maintenance of an accurate record of assets, dependencies and service topology, typically held in a configuration management database (CMDB).
5. **Resource and cost optimisation**. Capacity planning, performance tuning and control of cloud consumption. This includes to FinOps.

Modern ITOM is typically operated alongside site reliability engineering (SRE) and platform engineering practices. Responsibility for service reliability is shared between development and operations teams rather than held by a central operations function alone.

We believe these services are the foundation for an effectcive VMware optimstaion and migration plan. A migration plan depends on accurate data about the current estate: what is running, how much capacity it consumes, what it depends on and how it performs. ITOM tooling produces that data and provides the controls needed to move workloads without service degradation. Without it, migration outcomes will be at risk, as licence sizing, migration sequencing and post migration validation will be inaccurate.
