## Broadcom Product Description (Key Features) (This is important to get right)

CA Service Desk Manager (SDM) is the on-premises ITSM component of CA Service Management. CA Service Management 17.5 was released on 15 September 2026 (the documentation version list is 17.5, 17.4, 17.3 and 17.2). It adds a containerised Service Intelligence Platform, Virtual Analyst (ARIA) for analysts, semantic search and native analytics.

Key features:

1. **ITIL-aligned service management** - Incident, problem, change, request and knowledge management in a single platform, with the shared CA MDB configuration database.
2. **xFlow analyst experience** - xFlow supports customisable ticket forms via Form Designer, related ticket linking, suggested knowledge solutions while composing tickets, field-level change tracking and publishing resolutions as knowledge articles (17.5).
3. **AI-assisted service (17.5)** - A containerised Service Intelligence Platform provides chatbot and NLP-driven semantic search, and Virtual Analyst (ARIA) is extended from business users to analysts.
4. **Native analytics and modern platform support (17.5)** - Built-in reporting and dashboards with reporting APIs, Kerberos authentication as NTLM is phased out, IPv6 and HTTP/2 support, and support for dates beyond 2038.

## Analyst Cautions and Industry Findings - Related to the Legacy Broadcom Product. Include newest findings on Broadcom exist strategies

Analyst cautions and commercial pressures: Gartner Peer Insights rates CA Service Management, which bundles Service Desk Manager and Service Catalog, at 3.7 out of 5 from 95 verified reviews as at 2026, with one reviewer noting strong workflow automation and SLA monitoring alongside an interface that "feels less modern and should be upgraded." TrustRadius separately scores the same product 5.4 out of 10 from 49 reviews, with a reviewer stating that support "response times are exceedingly increasing to the point of waiting 24 hours for initial response," although another reviewer described the user community as responsive. The product remains on an on-premises architecture, with the latest generally available release line at 17.5 (released 15 September 2026, after the 17.4 line) and no Broadcom-delivered cloud-native ITSM successor identified, consistent with this row's existing claim of legacy technical debt. General Broadcom commercial pressures, including bundling and renewal price increases, are documented in the 2025 procurement sourcing literature cited throughout this review.

Broadcom exit strategies and market alternatives: ServiceNow ITSM and Atlassian Jira Service Management are established market leaders in Gartner's IT Service Management Platforms Peer Insights market. BMC Helix, recently spun off as an independent company according to CIO reporting, is a realistic direct competitor. IBM Maximo IT is a genuine ITSM offering tracked in the same Gartner market, but it is a considerably smaller player primarily known for enterprise asset management; it is a viable but niche alternative rather than a mainstream ITSM leader, and this should be made clear to the client.
