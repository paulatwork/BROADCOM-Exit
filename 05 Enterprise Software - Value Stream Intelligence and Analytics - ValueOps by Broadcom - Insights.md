## Broadcom Product Description - Key Features

ValueOps Insights is the value stream intelligence module of ValueOps by Broadcom. It gathers data from the tools that run value streams, organises it in a Common Data Model, and provides role-specific dashboards and automated reports with no coding required. It supplies enterprise-wide DORA and Flow measurements that go beyond team-level DevOps metrics.

Key features:

1. **DORA and Flow measurements** - Enterprise-wide DORA and Flow metrics act as leading indicators of delivery speed, quality and predictability.
2. **Common Data Model and multi-tool data integration** - Aggregates data from Clarity, Rally, ConnectALL and third-party tools into one model for consistent cross-tool reporting.
3. **Product Hierarchy Modeler** - Supports the transition from project-based to product-centric delivery by modelling products, value streams and teams.
4. **Prescriptive, role-specific dashboards** - Out-of-the-box real-time dashboards and automated reports tailored to each role, from developers to executives.

## Analyst Cautions and Industry Findings - Related to the Legacy Broadcom Product. Include newest findings on Broadcom exist strategies

ValueOps Insights has almost no independent footprint. No Gartner Peer Insights, Forrester or IDC coverage specific to it as a standalone product was found. Where it is assessed at all, it sits in the broader value stream management category next to ConnectALL, which itself has no recorded reviews. This absence should be stated plainly. It is not a sign of quality in either direction.

What can be said is structural. Insights draws its most useful numbers from Clarity and Rally delivery data, so an organisation that runs neither product gets much less from it. That is the platform lock-in pattern seen across Broadcom's former CA portfolio, and it means a customer leaving Clarity or Rally will almost certainly leave Insights at the same time.

No named organisation has publicly described leaving Insights on its own. For the analytics layer, IBM Apptio Targetprocess is a genuine, Gartner-tracked vendor in strategic portfolio management and agile analytics, Planview Viz is an actively marketed analytics product, and ServiceNow Strategic Portfolio Management is recognised as a Leader in recent Gartner and Forrester assessments. Allstacks is a small, venture-backed engineering analytics company with no material analyst coverage, so it should be presented as a niche option and not as a proven enterprise replacement.

## IBM Replacement Strategy - Why IBM over Broadcom

IBM Apptio Targetprocess provides a modern, cloud-native strategic portfolio and value stream management platform that outpaces Broadcom Insights on capability and total cost of ownership. Broadcom Insights functions primarily as a reporting add-on locked into the Clarity/Rally ecosystem, with Broadcom's Enterprise Agreements forcing expensive multi-product bundle renewals. In contrast, IBM Apptio Targetprocess natively models complex product hierarchies, aggregates delivery and financial data across heterogeneous toolchains (Jira, Azure DevOps, Rally, GitLab), and delivers turnkey Flow Metrics and OKR tracking to establish clear line-of-sight from strategic investment to execution.

## IBM PRIMARY - Product Description

IBM Apptio Targetprocess is IBM's enterprise Agile portfolio management and value stream management platform that connects business strategy to agile execution. It aggregates work and flow data across disparate engineering and business tools to deliver comprehensive value stream intelligence, custom configurable dashboards, and automated governance.

- **DORA and Flow measurements** — IBM Apptio Targetprocess natively calculates and visualizes Flow Framework metrics (Flow Velocity, Flow Efficiency, Flow Time, Flow Load, and Flow Distribution) alongside DevOps delivery cadence indicators across multi-team release trains and value streams.
- **Common Data Model and multi-tool data integration** — IBM Apptio Targetprocess features a flexible, unified domain data model that seamlessly ingests and synchronizes epics, user stories, defects, and tasks across heterogeneous toolsets including Jira, Azure DevOps, Rally, and GitHub without requiring proprietary platform lock-in.
- **Product Hierarchy Modeler** — IBM Apptio Targetprocess provides native multi-level portfolio and product hierarchy modeling that maps portfolios, value streams, solution trains, and Agile teams to support organizational shifts from project-based funding to product-centric delivery.
- **Prescriptive, role-specific dashboards** — IBM Apptio Targetprocess delivers out-of-the-box and deeply customizable role-based views and interactive dashboards tailored for executives, portfolio managers, product owners, and engineering leads with real-time analytics and automated status reporting.
