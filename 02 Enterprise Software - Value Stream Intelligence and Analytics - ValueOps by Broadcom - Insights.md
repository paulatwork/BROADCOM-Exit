## Broadcom Product Description - Key Features

In the ValueOps suite, Insights is analytics layer that collects data from Clarity and other delivery tools and measures whether execution is producing the intended outcomes. Clarity holds the approved plan; Insights measures performance against it.

Insights does not originate portfolio or financial transactions. It reads from source systems and derives metrics. It gathers data from the tools that run value streams, organises it in a Common Data Model, and provides role-specific dashboards and automated reports with no coding required. It supplies enterprise-wide DORA and Flow measurements that go beyond team-level DevOps metrics.

It is continuously correlating leading indicators with execution, operational and business performance data so that organisations executives can identify risk in project delivery and financial peformance. Its functions include measuring alignment between business goals and value stream delivery capability, so that misalignments representing risk to business outcomes can be identified.

Key features:

1. **DORA and Flow measurements** - Enterprise-wide DORA and Flow metrics act as leading indicators of delivery speed, quality and predictability.
2. **Common Data Model and multi-tool data integration** - Aggregates data from Clarity, Rally, ConnectALL and third-party tools into one model for consistent cross-tool reporting.
3. **Product Hierarchy Modeler** - Supports the transition from project-based to product-centric delivery by modelling products, value streams and teams.
4. **Prescriptive, role-specific dashboards** - Out-of-the-box real-time dashboards and automated reports tailored to each role, from developers to executives.

## IBM Replacement Strategy (Description - Why IBM over Broadcom)

IBM Targetprocess provides a modern, cloud-native strategic portfolio and value stream management platform that outpaces Broadcom Insights on capability and total cost of ownership. Broadcom Insights functions primarily as a reporting add-on locked into the Clarity/Rally ecosystem, with Broadcom's Enterprise Agreements forcing expensive multi-product bundle renewals. 

In contrast, IBM Targetprocess provies flow metrics, portfolio analytics and dashboarding capabilities. It models complex product hierarchies, aggregates delivery and financial data across heterogeneous toolchains (Jira, Azure DevOps, Rally, GitLab), and delivers turnkey Flow Metrics and OKR tracking to establish clear line-of-sight from strategic investment to execution.

Combine with IBM Apptio, the natural extension of Strategic Portfolio Management, adding total control and visiability of all IT investments and financial spend to the analysis. Together they close the loop from strategy to spend: Targetprocess aligns and plans the work and resources against strategic objectives, Apptio grounds that plan in defensible, integrated financial reality.

## IBM PRIMARY - Product Description

IBM Targetprocess is IBM's enterprise Agile portfolio management and value stream management platform that connects business strategy to agile execution. It aggregates work and flow data across disparate engineering and business tools to deliver comprehensive value stream intelligence, custom configurable dashboards, and automated governance.

- **DORA and Flow measurements** — IBM Targetprocess natively calculates and visualizes Flow Framework metrics (Flow Velocity, Flow Efficiency, Flow Time, Flow Load, and Flow Distribution) alongside DevOps delivery cadence indicators across multi-team release trains and value streams.
- **Common Data Model and multi-tool data integration** — IBM Targetprocess features a flexible, unified domain data model that seamlessly ingests and synchronizes epics, user stories, defects, and tasks across heterogeneous toolsets including Jira, Azure DevOps, Rally, and GitHub without requiring proprietary platform lock-in.
- **Product Hierarchy Modeler** — IBM Targetprocess provides native multi-level portfolio and product hierarchy modeling that maps portfolios, value streams, solution trains, and Agile teams to support organizational shifts from project-based funding to product-centric delivery.
- **Prescriptive, role-specific dashboards** — IBM Targetprocess delivers out-of-the-box and deeply customizable role-based views and interactive dashboards tailored for executives, portfolio managers, product owners, and engineering leads with real-time analytics and automated status reporting. Provides prescriptive, role-specific dashboards & automated reports via IBM Targetprocess and Apptio working together; gives role-tailored dashboards for flow metrics, OKRs and role-specific financial insight for CIOs/CFOs, business and service owners.
