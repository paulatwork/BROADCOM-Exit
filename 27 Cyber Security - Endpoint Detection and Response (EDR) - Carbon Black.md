## Broadcom Product Description (Key Features) (This is important to get right)

Carbon Black is Broadcom's endpoint security family, including Carbon Black Cloud (Endpoint Standard and Enterprise EDR) and the on-premises Carbon Black EDR. Container mode for Carbon Black EDR reaches end of support on 1 March 2027 (7.9.0 is the last version supporting it), and Broadcom announced Symantec CBX (a cloud XDR combining Symantec and Carbon Black technology) in March 2026.

Key features:

1. **Continuous endpoint recording** - Records endpoint activity so investigators can rewind an attack.
2. **Behavioural detection (NGAV)** - Endpoint Standard combines next-generation antivirus with behavioural EDR.
3. **Attack-chain visibility** - Root cause analysis and visualisation of the whole attack chain.
4. **Remote response** - Live response and remediation to contain threats.

## Analyst Cautions and Industry Findings - Related to the Legacy Broadcom Product. Include newest findings on Broadcom exist strategies

Broadcom's own direction is now Symantec CBX (Carbon Black XDR), announced in March 2026 to combine Symantec and Carbon Black technologies in one cloud XDR platform, and Carbon Black EDR Container mode reaches end of support on 1 March 2027. Broadcom decided in February 2024 not to sell Carbon Black and to merge it with Symantec.

Organisations reassessing endpoint detection and response in this environment are migrating toward cloud-native EDR/XDR platforms, principally CrowdStrike Falcon, Microsoft Defender for Endpoint, SentinelOne Singularity, and IBM Security QRadar EDR (formerly ReaQta).

Sources: ThreatDown, 'Carbon Black and Broadcom: Acquisition, Symantec, and what's next' (threatdown.com); BankInfoSecurity/GovInfoSecurity, 'Broadcom Axes Carbon Black Sale, to Merge Unit With Symantec' (bankinfosecurity.com); Gartner Peer Insights, Carbon Black EDR and Carbon Black Cloud product pages; Redress Compliance, 'Symantec Enterprise Software Licensing Under Broadcom: A CIO Playbook' (redresscompliance.com).
