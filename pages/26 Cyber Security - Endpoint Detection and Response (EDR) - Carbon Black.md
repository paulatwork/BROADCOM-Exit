## Broadcom Product Description - Key Features

Carbon Black is Broadcom's endpoint security family, including Carbon Black Cloud (Endpoint Standard and Enterprise EDR) and the on-premises Carbon Black EDR. Container mode for Carbon Black EDR reaches end of support on 1 March 2027 (7.9.0 is the last version supporting it), and Broadcom announced Symantec CBX (a cloud XDR combining Symantec and Carbon Black technology) in March 2026.

Key features:

1. **Continuous endpoint recording** - Records endpoint activity so investigators can rewind an attack.
2. **Behavioural detection (NGAV)** - Endpoint Standard combines next-generation antivirus with behavioural EDR.
3. **Attack-chain visibility** - Root cause analysis and visualisation of the whole attack chain.
4. **Remote response** - Live response and remediation to contain threats.

## Analyst Cautions and Industry Findings - Related to the Legacy Broadcom Product. Include newest findings on Broadcom exist strategies

Carbon Black has been through several changes of direction in a few years, and its customers felt each one. Broadcom decided in February 2024 not to sell it after all and to merge it with Symantec instead. In March 2026 Broadcom announced Symantec CBX, a cloud XDR platform that combines Symantec and Carbon Black technologies, and Carbon Black EDR Container mode reaches end of support on 1 March 2027. For a customer, that means the product is being folded into something new.

That uncertainty is what moved people. The clearest named case is Thomas H. Lee Partners, a private equity firm that invests in more than 35 mid-market companies. Through its managed detection and response provider, eSentire, it moved from Carbon Black to CrowdStrike Falcon. eSentire and CrowdStrike announced a partnership expansion in 2024 to take over protection of Carbon Black accounts, and eSentire reported migrating over a third of its customer base within a year. The reasons given were the uncertainty created by the sale attempt and merger, and CrowdStrike's research and development investment, and they came from vendor and provider accounts, not from an independent survey. SentinelOne also markets itself openly as the alternative that displaces Carbon Black and Symantec, which is a competitor's claim.

Organisations reassessing endpoint detection and response are choosing cloud-native platforms. CrowdStrike Falcon, Microsoft Defender for Endpoint, SentinelOne Singularity and IBM Security QRadar EDR, formerly ReaQta, are the main ones.

## IBM Replacement Strategy (Description - Why IBM over Broadcom)

IBM Security QRadar EDR (built on ReaQta behavioral AI technology) replaces Broadcom Carbon Black by delivering automated, lightweight endpoint detection, response, and investigation. Broadcom has vacillated on Carbon Black's strategy (announcing a sale, reversing it, forcing mergers with Symantec under Symantec CBX, and dropping container support), creating severe roadmap uncertainty and price increases. IBM QRadar EDR provides autonomous AI-based behavioral detection that blocks zero-day and ransomware attacks at the endpoint without relying on signature downloads, drastically reducing analyst alert fatigue and infrastructure overhead.

## IBM PRIMARY - Product Description

IBM Security QRadar EDR (formerly ReaQta) is IBM's endpoint detection and response solution designed to protect organizations from advanced cyber threats, ransomware, and fileless malware using automated behavioral intelligence and nanosecond-level response.

- **Continuous endpoint recording** — IBM Security QRadar EDR continuously captures and records low-level endpoint activities, process creations, and memory events using a dual-engine architecture, allowing security analysts to replay full attack sequences with zero telemetry loss.
- **Behavioural detection (NGAV)** — IBM Security QRadar EDR combines next-generation antivirus with autonomous behavioral AI that continuously monitors endpoint OS behaviors to detect and terminate anomalous, evasive, and fileless malware in real time.
- **Attack-chain visibility** — IBM Security QRadar EDR automatically maps detected activities to the MITRE ATT&CK framework, constructing an intuitive, visual attack-tree representation that isolates root causes and highlights impacted assets.
- **Remote response** — IBM Security QRadar EDR provides instant live terminal access and automated remote response capabilities that isolate compromised endpoints, terminate malicious processes, and delete malicious artifacts without disrupting business operations.
